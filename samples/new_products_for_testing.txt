======================================================================
HOW TO USE THIS FILE:
1. Open LetShop in your browser (index.html).
2. Click "Add Product" in the top navigation bar.
3. Copy and paste each field below into the corresponding form input.
4. For "Product Image", click "Choose File" and select the matching .jpg
   file located in this same "samples/" folder.
5. Click "Save Product".
6. Your new product will appear immediately on the home page!
